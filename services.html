What We Do

AV that fits your business — and your budget.

Most small businesses get quoted enterprise systems they don't need, or get handed a box and told good luck. We design, install, and support systems sized for how your space is actually used.

Conference Room AV

Meeting spaces that work on the first try.

Nobody should lose ten minutes of a meeting fighting an HDMI cable. We build conference rooms that turn on, connect, and just run.

  • Display selection and mounting sized to the room, not oversold
  • Video conferencing setup — cameras, microphones, and speakers that actually pick up the whole table
  • Room audio tuned so remote participants can hear clearly
  • Simple, single-button controls your whole team can use without training
  • Cable management and connectivity that stays tidy and out of the way

  • Screens and players installed and configured end to end

AV Infrastructure

The mounting, wiring, and sound behind it all.

The unglamorous part that determines whether everything else works. Done right the first time, so you're not calling someone back in six months.

  • Professional display and TV mounting — properly anchored, safely rated for the wall and screen
  • Background music and paging systems for restaurants, retail, and offices
  • Speaker placement and zoning so volume works across the whole space
  • Source switching and connectivity between displays, players, and devices
  • Cleanup and consolidation of existing setups that grew messy over time
Networking Setup

The network your AV actually depends on.

Digital signage, video conferencing, and streaming all live or die by the network behind them. We make sure that part isn't an afterthought.

  • Network switch setup and configuration
  • VLAN and port configuration to keep AV traffic separated and reliable
  • Wireless access point placement and configuration for solid coverage
  • Troubleshooting existing networks that drop, lag, or bottleneck
  • Coordination of structured cabling (Cat5e/Cat6) runs for new or expanding spaces

A note on cabling: structured cable runs are coordinated through trusted, licensed local contractors — either ours or your preferred contractor if you already have one. Switch configuration, network setup, and everything that happens after the cable is pulled is handled directly by us.
