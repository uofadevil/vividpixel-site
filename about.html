About Vivid Pixel

Run by one person who actually shows up.

Vivid Pixel is a Tucson-based digital signage service — no call center, no account rep who's never seen your business. When you work with Vivid Pixel, you're working directly with the person who plans, installs, and supports your screens.

The platform behind the scenes is built on proven, professional-grade signage technology — but branded and supported locally, so you get enterprise-level reliability with small-business responsiveness.

Vivid Pixel also draws on hands-on experience across both commercial and residential AV, so screen placement, wiring, and setup are handled with an eye for how spaces actually get used.
